# Quiz Review Criteria

Score each category 1-5 (1 = poor, 5 = excellent).

## 1. Game Mechanics (max 5)
- Is the core loop clear? (select → guess → feedback)
- Is difficulty balanced? (not too easy, not frustrating)
- Are hints progressive and useful?
- Does scoring feel fair?
- Is there a satisfying completion state?

## 2. Visual Design (max 5)
- Consistent with project dark theme?
- Accent color used effectively?
- Good visual hierarchy (what draws the eye first)?
- Animations/transitions smooth and purposeful?
- No visual clutter or overwhelming density?

## 3. Data Quality (max 5)
- Is the dataset interesting and well-known enough?
- Are relationships/connections meaningful?
- No duplicates or data errors?
- Reasonable quantity (not too few, not overwhelming)?
- Good variety within the dataset?

## 4. Usability (max 5)
- Can a new player understand what to do in <10 seconds?
- Are instructions present or is it self-explanatory?
- Feedback on correct/incorrect is immediate and clear?
- Can the player recover from mistakes?
- Is the search/filter functional and responsive?

## 5. Mobile Experience (max 5)
- Layout adapts to small screens?
- Touch targets are large enough (44px+)?
- No precision-dependent interactions?
- Text readable without zooming?
- No horizontal scroll or overflow issues?

## 6. Code Quality (max 5)
- Single self-contained HTML file?
- No unnecessary dependencies?
- Clean game state management?
- No console errors on load or during play?
- Reasonable file size (<500KB without assets)?

## 7. Accessibility (max 5)
- Keyboard navigable?
- Screen reader compatible (ARIA labels)?
- Color not sole indicator of state?
- Sufficient contrast ratios?
- Focus indicators visible?

## 8. Performance (max 5)
- Loads in <2 seconds on average connection?
- Smooth interactions (no jank)?
- Physics/animations don't drain battery?
- Stabilization completes in reasonable time?
- No memory leaks during extended play?

## 9. Fun Factor (max 5)
- Is it genuinely enjoyable to play?
- Does it teach or reveal something interesting?
- Would you share it with friends?
- Is there replay value?
- Does difficulty feel like a challenge, not a chore?

## 10. Polish (max 5)
- No rough edges or placeholder content?
- Consistent naming and messaging?
- Edge cases handled (empty states, all-correct, all-wrong)?
- Completion modal is satisfying?
- Overall feels "finished"?
